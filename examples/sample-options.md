# Spring Lab Science Options (sample)

> A made-up example of the options document `class-registration-planner` writes for an advisor meeting. The school, courses, CRNs and people are invented.

**Student:** working full time · **School:** Example State University · **Term:** Spring

## 1. Bottom line

The planned lab science (PHYS 201/202) isn't offered at a time that fits. The degree audit also accepts **PHYS 151/152**. Recommended: **PHYS 151 Lecture B (MW 8:00–9:15 AM) + Lab M 6:00–7:50 PM**. That's about 3 hours a week away from work, and back before the 10:00 meeting. Take PHYS 152 in a later term.

The audit's Lab Science rule accepts any **one** of these sequences. Sequences can't be mixed (PHYS 151 must be followed by PHYS 152).

| Sequence | Notes |
|---|---|
| PHYS 201 + 202 | On the plan. Daytime only this term |
| PHYS 151 + 152 | Accepted by the audit. Has an early-morning lecture and evening labs |
| CHEM 111 + 112 | Accepted. All labs are weekday afternoons |

## 7. Later-term and transfer options

- **Summer:** PHYS 151 is offered in a compressed 6-week daytime format. It doesn't fit work.
- **Community college:** an evening PHYS course may transfer as PHYS 151. **Confirm with the registrar before enrolling.** Nothing here assumes it will transfer.

## 8. Heads-up for future terms

Most required 300-level CS courses only meet 9:00–3:00 on weekdays. Plan now for one or two terms with more time away from work, or ask whether the department has evening or online sections.
